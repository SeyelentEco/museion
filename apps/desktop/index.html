Other content folders

Found something that isn't in the catalogue? Add the folder it's in. Archives (.zim) in it are served and searched like everything else; PDFs in it are indexed by page and cited like the rest. The catalogue is a starting point, not a boundary.

Models

Writes the answers. Chosen for your hardware tier; holds most of the GPU while running.
Reads each retrieved passage against your question and orders them by relevance. Small; runs on the CPU. Without it, answers still work but are ordered by keyword match alone.

Helper programs

Serves the archives for reading and searching. From the Kiwix project.
Runs the chat model and the reranker. From llama.cpp; llama-fit-params is expected beside it.

Two separate projects, so they are pointed at separately — if you already have either installed, use the copy you have.

Maintenance

Fetching content and adding folders already do both of these. They are here for what happens outside the app.

Indexes anything in your library or extra folders that isn't indexed yet — for archives or PDFs you copied in by hand, or a set-up that stopped before this step. Skips what's already done. Needs the services running.
Measures the chat model against your GPU and stores the layer split. Set-up did this once; run it again after changing the GPU or the chat model. Stops the chat model to measure, so start services afterwards.

Welcome to Museion

An offline reference library with a librarian who answers from it — and says so when it can't. Setting up needs internet once. After that, nothing does.

Set up this machine

Museion never chooses where things go — pick folders with room. It will show what it plans to fetch, and how large each piece is, before anything downloads.
